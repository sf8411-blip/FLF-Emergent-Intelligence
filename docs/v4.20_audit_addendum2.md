## Interpretation

The audit confirms three decisive instrument defects in the baseline implementation even though several exact counts from the proposed audit do not reproduce:

1. the gating trigger is effectively closed on the object actually passed by `run_seed`, so guided search is disabled by construction;
2. the baseline candidate language contains benchmark-specific hidden-operator templates rather than a compositional primitive grammar;
3. the control admission path can terminate before the intervention challenge on every tested decision, so the challenge was not stress-tested against hard false positives.

These findings invalidate the planned dress implementation at the audited commit. They do **not** alter the closed v4.19 A3 files or verdict. Repair work therefore proceeds only on DEV and only under R-A..R-F, with a fresh five-iteration instrument budget.

No mathematical-genesis or AGI claim is supported by this audit.
